  - Magnitude is close but not identical — mem2 runs consistently ~0.07–0.10 K

  bias from that single member's own internal variability.

  freezing), not a rounding footnote, and it's consistent between the two
  pipelines rather than being one-sided.

  Along the way I caught and fixed a wrong assumption in an early draft: I'd
  assumed the applied perturbation (perturbed − original) would be constant
  across every day within a calendar month, but that's only true where the 271 K
  floor doesn't kick in — floor-clamped cells depend on that day's own raw SST
  value. Fixed by comparing the intended stage-03 delta fields directly (loaded
  straight from each project's 03_delta/<month>.nc) rather than reconstructing
  them from perturbed-minus-original, which also made the whole comparison
  floor-independent and cleaner.
